# Stellar GreenPay - Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 2026

Stellar GreenPay is an open-source, non-custodial climate donation platform built on the Stellar network. We prioritize user privacy and minimize data collection.

---

## 1. Non-Custodial Architecture

Stellar GreenPay does not hold, custody, or manage private keys or funds:
- Transactions are signed locally via your chosen Stellar wallet (e.g. Freighter).
- Your private keys and secret seeds never leave your browser or device.
- We never have access to your private credentials.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Public Blockchain Data
When you make a donation or interact with smart contracts on the Stellar network:
- Your public wallet address, transaction amounts, timestamps, and project recipient details are broadcast to and recorded on the public Stellar ledger.
- This information is publicly accessible and permanent by the nature of blockchain technology.

### B. Browser Extension
The GreenPay Companion browser extension:
- Operates locally within your browser to detect Stellar public key addresses on visited pages.
- Does not collect, track, or sell your browsing history, personal data, or form inputs.
- Only communicates with the GreenPay platform when you explicitly interact with the extension to initiate a donation.

### C. Local Storage
- Settings, preferences, and recurring donation schedules may be stored locally on your device via `localStorage` or `AsyncStorage` for convenience and offline caching.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

GreenPay may interact with third-party services:
- **Stellar Horizon / Soroban RPC Nodes:** To submit transactions and read on-chain ledger state.
- **IPFS Gateways:** For decentralized storage and retrieval of project impact certificates and documentation.

These services have their own privacy policies governing data sent to their network endpoints.

---

## 4. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in functionality or applicable laws. Changes are effective upon updating this document in our repository.
